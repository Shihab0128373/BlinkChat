Private room for text and voice. Nothing is saved.

or join a friend's room

Privacy and limits

Messages live only in memory and vanish when you leave or refresh. Chat and calls go directly between browsers (WebRTC, encrypted in transit), but this app adds no end-to-end encryption of its own and cannot stop anyone from screenshotting or copying.

A free public PeerJS signaling server helps browsers find each other and can see connection metadata (IP addresses, Room ID). It may be slow or down. Some networks need a TURN relay for voice. The person who created the room is the host: if they leave, the room ends.
